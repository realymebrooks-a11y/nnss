@title Creep Mechanisms, Transmission-Line Failure Cases and Introduction to Fracture ||| 蠕变机理、输电线路失效案例与断裂导论
@summary 本讲先说明蠕变的主要机理是扩散：原子借助空位跳动，高温提供能量，外加应力使原子和空位的流动有了方向，从而产生变形；扩散还可以沿晶界进行；并讨论抗蠕变材料。然后通过输电线路设备（铁塔、导线、金具、绝缘子）的质量问题统计，以及热镀锌铁塔塔脚腐蚀和地线金具磨损两个工程案例，说明腐蚀、磨损等失效在实际中的表现及防护措施。最后引入断裂：断裂的概念、术语、断裂发生的尺度，以及脆性与韧性材料的应力—应变曲线。

@slide 4
How does creep happen? ||| 蠕变是如何发生的？
• One of the main mechanisms of creep is by diffusion: ||| • 蠕变的主要机理之一是<b>扩散</b>：
– Movement of atoms through the structure of a material ||| – 原子在材料结构中的移动
– In crystalline materials this is facilitated by vacancies ||| – 在晶体材料中，扩散借助<b>空位</b>进行
• Each vacancy allows a neighbouring atom to 'jump' between atomic sites ||| • 每个空位都允许相邻的原子在原子位置之间“跳跃”
• But atoms need a little help… ||| • 但原子需要一点“帮助”……

@slide 5
Lattice diffusion (1) ||| 晶格扩散（1）
• Thermal energy allows atoms to move via vacancies ||| • <b>热能</b>使原子能够借助空位移动
• Under no load, movement is completely random ||| • 不受载荷时，原子的移动是<b>完全随机</b>的
• No overall change ||| • 宏观上没有变化
High temperature ||| 高温

@slide 6
Lattice diffusion (2) ||| 晶格扩散（2）
• Applied loads add directionality to atomic movements ||| • 外加载荷使原子的移动有了<b>方向性</b>
• Creates a flux of mass and vacancies ||| • 形成质量流和空位流
• Results in deformation ||| • 从而产生<b>变形</b>
σ; High temperature ||| 应力 σ；高温
! 受拉方向的晶界附近空位较多，原子从受压的面扩散到受拉的面，晶粒沿受拉方向慢慢“拉长”——这就是扩散蠕变。温度越高，扩散越快，蠕变速率呈指数增大。

@slide 7
Other diffusion paths ||| 其他扩散途径
• Where else might diffusion occur in polycrystalline materials? ||| • 在多晶材料中，扩散还可能在哪里发生？
• Grain boundaries! ||| • <b>晶界</b>！
! 晶界处原子排列混乱、较疏松，扩散比晶粒内部快得多。所以晶粒越细（晶界越多），扩散蠕变越快——这与室温下“细晶强化”正好相反。

Summary ||| 小结
• It is important to consider length scales when discussing material properties and behaviour: – Being able to link microscopic and macroscopic behaviour is key ||| • 讨论材料性能和行为时必须考虑<b>尺度</b>：– 能把微观和宏观行为联系起来是关键
• Plastic flow occurs by shear in polycrystalline materials, with the maximum stress acting at 45° to an applied tensile load ||| • 多晶材料中<b>塑性流动通过剪切发生</b>，最大切应力作用在与拉伸载荷成 45° 的平面上
• At the onset of yielding, materials behave in one of two ways: – Increasing in strength, due to strain hardening – By displaying plastic instability (i.e. necking) ||| • 开始屈服后，材料有两种表现：– 由于<b>应变硬化</b>而强度提高 – 出现<b>塑性失稳</b>（即颈缩）
• Creep is the deformation that occurs at high temperatures under relatively low applied stresses – one of the key mechanisms by which it occurs is diffusion ||| • <b>蠕变</b>是在<b>高温</b>和相对<b>较低应力</b>下发生的变形——其关键机理之一是<b>扩散</b>

Protective measures ||| 防护措施
• The principle is to interrupt the relevant links that are beneficial to the corrosion process of the tower feet. ||| • 原则是<b>切断</b>有利于塔脚腐蚀过程的相关环节。
• Apply a protective layer with a thickness of about 1 mm from the upper to lower 50 mm at the connection interface between the tower foot and the concrete foundation, which can isolate the corroded parts from the corrosive medium. ||| • 在塔脚与混凝土基础的连接界面上下各 50 mm 范围内，涂覆约 1 mm 厚的防护层，把易腐蚀部位与腐蚀介质隔离。
• Make the concrete protective cap beveled to prevent dust from accumulating in the root of the tower. ||| • 把混凝土保护帽做成斜面，防止灰尘在塔脚根部堆积。
• Keep regular inspections and remove dust and salt accumulations in the tower feet timely. ||| • 定期巡检，及时清除塔脚处积聚的灰尘和盐分。
• All measures above should have been applied to the towers of some transmission lines for more than one year. ||| • 上述措施已在部分输电线路的铁塔上应用一年以上。

How do crystalline materials fail? ||| 晶体材料如何失效？
Plastic deformation (low temperature yielding, high temperature creep); Fracture (brittle, ductile); Fatigue (slow crack growth leading to fracture); Oxidation and Corrosion (reactions with O₂, and O₂ + H₂O); Wear (all of the above, with relative motion between parts!) ||| 塑性变形（低温屈服、高温蠕变）；断裂（脆性、韧性）；疲劳（裂纹缓慢扩展导致断裂）；氧化与腐蚀（与 O₂ 及 O₂ + H₂O 反应）；磨损（以上各种机制，再加上零件间的相对运动！）

@slide 23
Fracture concepts ||| 断裂的概念
• Fracture: ||| • 断裂：
– Initiation and rapid propagation of cracks within a material ||| – 材料内部裂纹的<b>萌生</b>和<b>快速扩展</b>
– Structure no longer sustains any applied loading ||| – 结构不再能承受任何外加载荷
– Often occurs at nominal stresses/strains below those that the material is expected to sustain – need to understand why this is! ||| – 往往在<b>低于</b>材料预期能承受的名义应力/应变时发生——需要弄清原因！
• Design against fracture: ||| • 防断裂设计：
– Crack-free materials? Very difficult to produce ||| – 无裂纹的材料？很难生产
– Therefore, we design for non-propagation or controlled propagation of cracks – this links in with fatigue behaviour ||| – 因此设计时要保证裂纹<b>不扩展</b>或<b>可控地扩展</b>——这与疲劳行为相关
– Need to select materials with sufficient toughness ||| – 需要选择具有足够<b>韧性</b>的材料

@slide 24
Fracture jargon ||| 断裂术语
• Materials may be: – Strong – Stiff – Tough – Ductile – Brittle… ||| • 材料可以是：– 强的 – 刚的 – 韧的 – 延性好的 – 脆的……
• Components may fail by: ||| • 零件可能的失效方式：
– Inelastic deformation – i.e. by yielding or creep ||| – <b>非弹性变形</b>——即屈服或蠕变
– Fracture – i.e. brittle or ductile, or following fatigue ||| – <b>断裂</b>——脆性或韧性断裂，或疲劳后断裂
– Corrosion – can cause failures independently or contribute to the above ||| – <b>腐蚀</b>——可以单独导致失效，也可以促进上述失效
– Wear – all failure mechanisms contribute to wear mechanisms ||| – <b>磨损</b>——各种失效机制都对磨损有贡献
! 区分：strong（强，抵抗屈服/断裂的应力高）、stiff（刚，E 大，变形小）、tough（韧，断裂前吸收能量多、抗裂纹扩展）、ductile（延性好，断裂前塑性变形大）、brittle（脆，几乎无塑性变形就断裂）。玻璃“刚而脆”，橡胶“韧而不刚”。

@example 温度对扩散蠕变速率的影响 Effect of temperature on creep rate
Q: Diffusion (and hence diffusion creep rate) follows an Arrhenius law, rate ∝ exp(−Q/RT). For a nickel alloy with activation energy Q = 280 kJ mol⁻¹, by what factor does the creep rate increase when the blade temperature rises from 900 °C to 950 °C? (R = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹) ||| 扩散（以及扩散蠕变速率）服从阿伦尼乌斯规律：速率 ∝ exp(−Q/RT)。某镍合金的激活能 Q = 280 kJ/mol，叶片温度从 900 °C 升高到 950 °C 时，蠕变速率增大多少倍？（R = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹）
S: 换算成开尔文：T₁ = 1173 K，T₂ = 1223 K
S: 速率比 = exp[(Q/R)(1/T₁ − 1/T₂)]
S: 1/T₁ − 1/T₂ = 1/1173 − 1/1223 = 8.525 × 10⁻⁴ − 8.177 × 10⁻⁴ = 3.49 × 10⁻⁵ K⁻¹
S: Q/R = 280 000 / 8.314 = 33 678 K
S: 速率比 = exp(33 678 × 3.49 × 10⁻⁵) = exp(1.175) ≈ <b>3.2</b>
A: 仅升高 50 °C，蠕变速率约增大到 3.2 倍。所以涡轮叶片要用内部冷却通道和热障涂层，哪怕只降低几十度，寿命也能显著延长。

@example 塔脚腐蚀减薄后的承载能力 Load capacity after corrosion thinning
Q: A tower-foot angle member originally has a cross-section of 1200 mm² and is designed to carry 150 kN in tension (σ_y = 345 MPa). Corrosion (slides 15–16) has reduced the effective cross-section by 30%. (a) Find the safety factor against yielding before and after corrosion. (b) What is the minimum section that keeps a safety factor of 1.5? ||| 某塔脚角钢原截面积 1200 mm²，设计承受 150 kN 拉力（σ_y = 345 MPa）。腐蚀（第 15–16 页）使有效截面积减少了 30%。(a) 求腐蚀前后防屈服的安全系数；(b) 要保持安全系数 1.5，最小截面积是多少？
S: (a) 腐蚀前：σ = 150 000 / 1200 = 125 MPa；安全系数 n = 345 / 125 = <b>2.76</b>
S: 腐蚀后：A = 1200 × 0.70 = 840 mm²；σ = 150 000 / 840 = 178.6 MPa；n = 345 / 178.6 = <b>1.93</b>
S: (b) 要 n ≥ 1.5：许用应力 = 345 / 1.5 = 230 MPa → A_min = 150 000 / 230 = <b>652 mm²</b>，即最多允许损失约 46% 的截面。
A: 安全系数从 2.76 降到 1.93。腐蚀坑还会造成应力集中，并成为疲劳裂纹源，实际危险比单纯截面减薄更大——所以要定期巡检和采取防护措施。

@example 判断失效模式 Identifying the failure mode
Q: Using slides 22–24, identify the main failure mechanism and one suitable countermeasure for: (a) a wire strand that breaks after years of wind-induced vibration; (b) a U-shackle worn into a groove; (c) a lead-sheathed cable that slowly sags between supports; (d) a glass insulator that shatters suddenly on a cold night; (e) a galvanised tower leg thinned near the ground. ||| 根据第 22–24 页，判断下列情况的主要失效机制，并各提出一项对策：(a) 导线股线在多年风振后断裂；(b) U 形挂环磨出沟槽；(c) 铅护套电缆在支点间慢慢下垂；(d) 玻璃绝缘子在寒冷的夜晚突然碎裂；(e) 镀锌铁塔腿部靠近地面处减薄。
S: (a) <b>疲劳</b>：循环应力低于屈服强度，裂纹缓慢扩展后断裂 → 安装防振锤，降低振幅。
S: (b) <b>磨损</b>：小接触面、大活动空间，反复碰撞和滑动 → 改用直角挂环（面接触）或耐磨材料。
S: (c) <b>蠕变</b>：铅在室温下 T/Tₘ ≈ 0.49 → 缩短支点间距，或改用抗蠕变的护套材料。
S: (d) <b>脆性断裂</b>：玻璃没有塑性变形，温差产生的热应力在微小缺陷处引发裂纹快速扩展 → 采用钢化玻璃或复合（硅橡胶）绝缘子。
S: (e) <b>腐蚀</b>：镀锌层消耗后钢基体被腐蚀 → 涂覆防护层、斜面保护帽、定期清理积盐（第 17 页）。
A: 疲劳、磨损、蠕变、脆性断裂、腐蚀——课程中讨论的五类失效机制在一条输电线路上都能找到实例。
