Case study ||| 案例
(Diagram) Cantilever, length L, end load F_D, section b × d ||| （图）悬臂梁，长 L，端部载荷 F_D，截面 b × d
For a given material the material index for strong light beam: √σ_f / ρ = C ||| 对于给定材料，轻而强的梁的材料指数为：√σ_f / ρ = C
Taking logs gives the expression: ½ log₁₀ σ_f − log₁₀ ρ = log₁₀ C ||| 取对数得：½ log₁₀ σ_f − log₁₀ ρ = log₁₀ C
Which is of the form y = mx + c ||| 这正是直线方程 y = mx + c 的形式
So we can now plot log₁₀ σ_f versus log₁₀ ρ and plot guidelines with gradient m = 2 ||| 因此可以以 log₁₀ σ_f 对 log₁₀ ρ 作图，并画出斜率 m = 2 的导引线
(Chart) Materials property 2 vs Materials property 1; Guidelines for √σ_f/ρ = C; C = 40; Search Region ||| （图）材料性能 2 对材料性能 1；√σ_f/ρ = C 的导引线；C = 40；搜索区域
! 整理一下：log σ_f = 2 log ρ + 2 log C。所有 C 相同的材料落在同一条斜率为 2 的直线上；把直线向左上方平移，C 增大，直线上方（左上）区域就是“比这条线更好”的搜索区域。

@slide 6
Exam question 2013 ||| 2013 年考题
At the Battle of Bosworth in 1485, the best armour was almost impervious to arrows. ||| 1485 年博斯沃思战役中，最好的盔甲几乎能挡住弓箭。
Arrow shafts are designed to be light and stiff so they can be fired over a long distance. ||| 箭杆被设计得又轻又刚，这样才能射得远。
Historically, the shaft of an arrow was made from wood, often poplar but many other woods were used. ||| 历史上，箭杆用木材制作，常用杨木，也用过许多其他木材。
You are required to compare and contrast modern materials to form the shaft of an arrow. ||| 要求你比较各种现代材料用作箭杆的优劣。
The arrow shaft is a rod of length L which is fixed for any given archer. ||| 箭杆是一根长度为 L 的杆，对某一射手而言 L 是固定的。
The mass of the arrow is critical since a lighter arrow can be fired further, however, at the release of the arrow and the impact in its target, a high force is experienced and buckling must be resisted if the arrow is to find its target with accuracy and inflict damage. ||| 箭的质量至关重要，因为越轻的箭射得越远；但是在箭射出和击中目标的瞬间，箭会承受很大的力，必须防止屈曲，箭才能准确命中目标并造成伤害。
The elastic buckling load of a column of length L and radius r is F_crit = π²EI / L² ||| 长度为 L、半径为 r 的柱的弹性屈曲载荷为 F_crit = π²EI / L²
where E is the modulus and I is the second moment of the area of the column given by I = πr⁴/4 ||| 其中 E 为模量，I 为柱截面的惯性矩：I = πr⁴/4
Derive a suitable merit index which would allow you to minimise the weight of the arrow subject to the arrow shaft not buckling. ||| 推导一个合适的性能指数，使箭杆在不屈曲的前提下重量最小。
It is also desirable that the shaft should be as narrow as possible, as thicker arrows will exhibit greater air resistance. Derive a second merit index where the arrow should be as thin as possible without buckling. ||| 箭杆还应尽可能细，因为粗箭的空气阻力更大。推导第二个性能指数，使箭杆在不屈曲的前提下尽可能细。

@slide 7
Exam question 2013: Answer ||| 2013 年考题：解答
The requirement is to minimise the mass ||| 要求是使质量最小
Mass of a shaft is given by: m = πr²Lρ   (1) ||| 杆的质量：m = πr²Lρ　　(1)
Elastic buckling load of a column of length L and radius r is: F_crit = π²EI / L²   (2) ||| 长 L、半径 r 的柱的弹性屈曲载荷：F_crit = π²EI / L²　　(2)
Where I is the second moment of area: I = πr⁴/4   (3) ||| 其中 I 为截面惯性矩：I = πr⁴/4　　(3)
The load must not exceed F_crit. Insert (3) into (2): F ≤ π³Er⁴ / 4L²   (4) ||| 载荷不得超过 F_crit。把 (3) 代入 (2)：F ≤ π³Er⁴ / 4L²　　(4)

@slide 8
Exam question 2013: Answer ||| 2013 年考题：解答
If we rearrange equation (1) and square it we get an expression…: r⁴ = m² / π²L²ρ²   (5) ||| 把式 (1) 变形并平方，得到：r⁴ = m² / π²L²ρ²　　(5)
Insert (5) into (4): F ≤ π³Em² / 4L⁴π²ρ² ||| 把 (5) 代入 (4)：F ≤ π³Em² / 4L⁴π²ρ²
Rearranging for m, which we wish to minimise. If the arrow is not going to buckle then: m ≥ (4F/π)<sup>1/2</sup> L² [ρ / E<sup>1/2</sup>] ||| 解出我们希望最小化的 m。若箭不屈曲，则：m ≥ (4F/π)<sup>1/2</sup> L² [ρ / E<sup>1/2</sup>]
Design | Materials properties ||| 设计项 | 材料性能项

@slide 9
Exam question 2013: Answer ||| 2013 年考题：解答
All materials properties in square brackets. So if we want to minimise m then we need to maximise: M₁ = E<sup>1/2</sup> / ρ ||| 所有材料性能都在方括号内。所以要使 m 最小，就要使 M₁ = E<sup>1/2</sup> / ρ 最大
To minimise the cross sectional area of the shaft we simply need to rearrange: F ≤ π³Er⁴ / 4L² ||| 要使箭杆截面最小，只需把 F ≤ π³Er⁴ / 4L² 变形：
r ≥ (4F/π³)<sup>1/4</sup> (L)<sup>1/2</sup> [1/E]<sup>1/4</sup> ||| r ≥ (4F/π³)<sup>1/4</sup> (L)<sup>1/2</sup> [1/E]<sup>1/4</sup>
Again, materials properties are in square brackets. To minimise r we should maximise: M₂ = E ||| 同样，材料性能在方括号内。要使 r 最小，应使 M₂ = E 最大
! 两个指数对应两个不同目标：目标是“质量最小”→ M₁ = E<sup>1/2</sup>/ρ；目标是“直径最小”→ M₂ = E（与密度无关）。同一个约束（不屈曲）在不同目标下给出不同的材料指数。

@example 用两个指数比较箭杆材料 Comparing arrow-shaft materials
Q: Using typical values — wood (along grain): E = 10 GPa, ρ = 0.5 Mg m⁻³; aluminium alloy: E = 70 GPa, ρ = 2.7; steel: E = 210 GPa, ρ = 7.8; CFRP: E = 110 GPa, ρ = 1.55 — evaluate M₁ = E<sup>1/2</sup>/ρ and M₂ = E for each, and recommend an arrow-shaft material. ||| 取典型值——木材（顺纹）：E = 10 GPa，ρ = 0.5 Mg m⁻³；铝合金：E = 70 GPa，ρ = 2.7；钢：E = 210 GPa，ρ = 7.8；CFRP：E = 110 GPa，ρ = 1.55——计算各材料的 M₁ = E<sup>1/2</sup>/ρ 和 M₂ = E，并推荐箭杆材料。
S: M₁（质量最小）：木材 √10/0.5 = 3.16/0.5 = <b>6.32</b>；铝 √70/2.7 = 8.37/2.7 = <b>3.10</b>；钢 √210/7.8 = 14.5/7.8 = <b>1.86</b>；CFRP √110/1.55 = 10.5/1.55 = <b>6.77</b>（单位 GPa<sup>1/2</sup>/(Mg m⁻³)）
S: M₂（直径最小）：钢 210 ＞ CFRP 110 ＞ 铝 70 ＞ 木材 10（GPa）
S: 比较：钢 M₂ 最大但 M₁ 最小——箭杆最细但最重；木材 M₁ 很高（轻），但 M₂ 很低——箭杆粗、空气阻力大；CFRP 的 M₁ 最高，M₂ 也较高，两项都靠前。
A: 推荐 CFRP：同时满足“轻”和“细”，正好落在第 10 页图中的阴影区内。木材是“轻但粗”的传统选择，铝合金箭杆介于两者之间。

@example 推导轻而刚的拉杆的材料指数 Index for a light, stiff tie
Q: A tie of fixed length L carries a tensile force F, and its extension must not exceed δ. The cross-sectional area A is free. Derive the material index that minimises the mass, and state the gradient of its guideline on a log E – log ρ chart. ||| 一根长度 L 固定的拉杆承受拉力 F，伸长量不得超过 δ，截面积 A 可以自由选择。推导使质量最小的材料指数，并说明它在 log E – log ρ 图上导引线的斜率。
S: <b>目标</b>：质量 m = A L ρ　　(1)
S: <b>约束</b>（刚度）：δ = F L / (A E) ≤ δ_max → A ≥ F L / (E δ_max)　　(2)
S: <b>消去自由变量 A</b>：把 (2) 代入 (1)：m ≥ (F L² / δ_max) × [ρ / E]
S: 括号前为设计项，括号内为材料项。要 m 最小，就要使 <b>M = E / ρ</b> 最大（比刚度）。
S: 取对数：log E = log ρ + log M → 斜率为 <b>1</b> 的直线。
A: 指数 M = E/ρ，导引线斜率为 1。比较：拉杆 E/ρ（斜率 1）、屈曲的杆 E<sup>1/2</sup>/ρ（斜率 2）、受弯的板 E<sup>1/3</sup>/ρ（斜率 3）——同样“轻而刚”，受力形式不同，指数也不同。

@example 在对数图上画导引线 Plotting a guideline
Q: On a modulus–density chart, draw the guideline M₁ = E<sup>1/2</sup>/ρ = 6 (E in GPa, ρ in Mg m⁻³). Find two points on it, and decide whether a material with E = 50 GPa, ρ = 1.0 Mg m⁻³ lies above or below the line. ||| 在模量—密度图上画出导引线 M₁ = E<sup>1/2</sup>/ρ = 6（E 以 GPa 计，ρ 以 Mg m⁻³ 计）。求线上两点，并判断 E = 50 GPa、ρ = 1.0 Mg m⁻³ 的材料在线的上方还是下方。
S: 由 E<sup>1/2</sup> = 6ρ 得 E = 36ρ²。
S: 取 ρ = 1 → E = 36 GPa；取 ρ = 0.5 → E = 9 GPa；取 ρ = 2 → E = 144 GPa。在对数图上，ρ 每增大 10 倍，E 增大 100 倍——斜率为 2。
S: 判断：该材料 M₁ = √50 / 1.0 = 7.07 ＞ 6，或者看 ρ = 1 时它的 E = 50 ＞ 36，所以位于导引线<b>上方</b>（左上方），优于导引线上的材料。
A: 线上的点如 (0.5, 9)、(1, 36)、(2, 144)；该材料在导引线上方，属于搜索区域内的候选材料。
